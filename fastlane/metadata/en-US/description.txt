Curve is the grade tracker that answers the one question every student asks in December: what do I need on the final?

Type your syllabus once. Log each score as it comes back. Curve keeps the real weighted grade, shows where it is heading, and turns the dial to the score you need on whatever is left.

TERM
• Every course on one page with the current grade, letter, a trend arrow from your last three scores, and the next thing due
• Term GPA, weighted GPA and cumulative GPA at the top

COURSES THAT MATCH THE SYLLABUS
• Weighted categories or total points
• Drop the lowest N in any category
• Your own letter scale per course, with plus and minus grades
• Honors and AP bumps for the weighted GPA
• Quick setup: type "Homework 20, Quizzes 15, Midterm 25, Final 40" and it is done

WHAT DO I NEED
• Pick a target letter or slide to any percent
• Curve solves for the score you need on everything still ungraded
• It tells you plainly when a grade is already locked in, and when a target is out of reach
• A table of every letter and what each one takes

HONEST MATHS
• Categories with nothing graded yet are left out and the rest re-weighted, and Curve says so
• A running grade chart per course, so you see the curve, not just the number

GPA
• Per term and cumulative, unweighted and weighted
• What-if: try a different finish for each course and watch the projection move
• Editable points per letter

REPORT
• A clean one-page term report as a PDF, and a CSV of every score

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
